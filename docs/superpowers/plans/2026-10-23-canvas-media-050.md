## Design and scope

Reuse the current Android thumbnail/Ink-page renderers and board-coordinate CanvasCardFrame. A compact native header plus weighted media viewport replaces fixed110dp thumbnail rows; one source renders per card. Tapping the image selects the card, while a labelled inspection action opens a read-only full-screen fit/zoom/pan viewer. Existing Open source and Ink edit owners remain. PDF inspection can change the viewed page without changing annotations. Detailed graphics modification belongs to #51.

A large project may have hundreds of visible/overlapping cards, so viewport culling alone is insufficient. Admit at most16 visible thumbnails with focused-card priority, use512px bounded decodes and recycle after culling/source replacement. A shared host decode semaphore permits at most2 in-flight decodes. Explicit inspection alone may render up to1536px. This is a rendering budget, not a new cache/data/execution owner. Deferred cards retain their source/title and can be selected to show a preview.

Keep route, node position/size/revision, files, ownership/permission and markup storage unchanged. Source identity/version resets inspection. Normalized overlay and background use the exact same fitted geometry. Fit preserves extreme aspect ratios without cropping; unusual ratios may letterbox. No full-resolution bitmap per card and no decode on board pan/zoom.
