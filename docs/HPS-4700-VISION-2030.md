# HPS-4700: Vision 2030

**If Halo succeeds beyond our expectations, what will the world look like in five years?**

Halo will no longer be seen as an "AI assistant." It will be recognized as the ubiquitous **middleware that separates wearable hardware from AI intelligence**. 

Just as Android abstracted cameras, Bluetooth, and sensors behind stable APIs for mobile phones, Halo abstracts speech engines, AI providers, wearable devices, context, memory, and automation behind stable, inspectable, and privacy-first interfaces for the physical AI era.

In 2030:
* **Hardware Manufacturers** build glasses, earpieces, and pins that simply implement the Halo Driver SDK. They don't need to build an AI backend.
* **AI Model Developers** deploy new local and cloud models that instantly plug into the Halo Provider SDK, gaining access to millions of devices without writing client apps.
* **Plugin Authors** write capabilities once, knowing they will run securely on any form factor, automatically adapting to the user's attention state and context.
* **Users** own their data, inspect every decision their AI makes, and seamlessly swap providers, hardware, and plugins without vendor lock-in.

Halo is the open, secure, and resilient platform that makes the next generation of wearable intelligence possible.
