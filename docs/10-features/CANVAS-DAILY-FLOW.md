# Capture to Inbox, then organize on Canvas

From Home, open the actions menu and choose **Save to Inbox**. Workspace has the same button; Canvas has it under **Quick add**. Save a short note, import a file, take a photo or record a memo. Nothing is sent to AI automatically. For a memo, allow the microphone, then use **Stop & save**. **Cancel recording** discards it. Stop before leaving the screen; recording does not continue in the background.

Everything goes into the same Workspace Inbox. On Canvas, choose **Add to current Canvas** after saving, or Quick add → link an existing Workspace item. Boards reuse the saved item, so placing it on another board does not copy the file again. Android's Sharesheet can send several files to Halo; the receipt tells you how many were saved and how many failed.

Select an audio card and open **Card actions**. **Transcribe locally** uses your installed speech model and selected language from Voice Settings. If it is missing or cannot read the recording, use **Write transcript**. Review and edit the text before saving. The saved text becomes an Inbox note linked to the audio and board; the recording stays unchanged. Recognition accuracy varies, including Russian and Latvian. For an image/PDF, recognize page 1; handwriting and Cyrillic may not be readable. Check every result against the source.

**Search this canvas** finds cards, linked notes, tags and reviewed text. Choose All, Notes or Files, then tap a result to bring its card into view. Workspace search also finds this content. Use **Annotate** or **Edit sketch** to draw; saved marks belong to the board, not the original file. After an interruption, **Resume drawing** offers the recovered draft. Cancel discards that draft.

To organize manually, choose **Select cards**, tap the cards you want, then **Organize selected**. Edit group titles and memberships, or add optional next steps. **Approve organization** arranges the cards, connects the groups and can create a linked checklist note. **Cancel** leaves the board unchanged. **Undo** reverses the latest supported board operation while original notes/files remain saved. Card actions also offer tag/link/arrange/bring forward/ungroup/remove; removing a card does not delete its source.

**Ask Halo to organize** is optional. It makes a proposal that still needs your review and approval. Workspace privacy settings apply to every source and the board. Suggested next steps are not completed tasks and do not create calendar entries, notifications or actions in other apps.

On supported connected HeyCyan glasses, existing photo capture/review and explicit photo synchronization can save confirmed images to Inbox. A disconnected camera or failed transfer is a failure, not a saved photo. Generic wearable voice memo capture is not supported by this flow.
