QA55-T2 Water Cycle
This lesson has four stages: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.
Evaporation changes liquid water into water vapor when energy heats it.
Condensation changes water vapor into liquid droplets as it cools. These droplets form clouds.
Precipitation returns water from clouds as rain or snow.
Collection gathers water in rivers, lakes, and oceans. The cycle then repeats.
Recall question: Which stage changes water vapor into liquid droplets?
Expected answer: condensation.
