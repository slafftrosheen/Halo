## Contract decisions for implementation

1. **Screen is the everyday organizer home.** `docs/design/halo-zone5/WORKSPACE-TOOL-CONTRACT.md:50–52` already assigns calendar, plans/time management, notifications, weather, clock and alarms to Screen. Preserve the five Workspace instrument families at lines 7–15. Organizer subtools belong under Screen; Library exposes saved organizer records and their canonical routes. Reuse existing Notes, with references between records rather than a second note store. A local organizer chooser is working content; it is not a new global navigation bar.
2. **The current amended UI 5.0 contract governs.** The old four-family/thin-orb examples in `DESIGN.md` are explicitly superseded by the Workspace contract. Use its measured normal quarter-height Zone, work-only upper area, compact/IME and large-text fallback, classic thick orb and current theme roles. New screens bind one `ShellPageOwner` and current action lease; normal menus, satellites, pins, Help and voice reach the same domain operations. The shell owns navigation and identity. Avoid a second Scaffold top bar or toolbar above the working content.
3. **There are exactly two global execution/privacy modes, Local and Power.** No per-widget or source mode, Kids/Study privacy mode, or model-setup “limited mode” is a third choice. Model readiness, offline connectivity, source selection and OS permission are separate capability states. A source may be selected while Local; selection must not fetch. Power enables ordinary selected-source operations without another cloud-consent prompt. OS permission, account authentication and action-specific destructive confirmation remain distinct. The parent owns the execution-boundary/policy audit; UI labels must consume that same policy state.
4. **Fresh Local installation must provide a useful route immediately.** Organizer forms, clock, local tasks/reminders, Notes and deterministic Your Day must work without an answer model. Initial cards use actual local state and honest empty states. First-run source cards show bundled catalogue metadata, supported language/category, saved selection and manual-city choices; live preview/validation is only available under Power. A disabled preview must explain that boundary without blocking local completion. Preserve the existing setup opt-out and resume state.
5. **A saved record and its scheduled delivery are different outcomes.** An alarm/reminder can be saved while notification delivery or exact scheduling is unavailable. Display the owner receipt: saved, scheduled with its precision, permission needed, approximate, failed, snoozed, cancelled or completed as appropriate. Show the next occurrence with date/time and relevant time zone; recurrence edit/delete must state the chosen scope. Do not label an OEM editor launch as Created, or an external Clock request as Halo-scheduled. Calendar conflicts are indications, not silent rejection or rescheduling.
6. **Source and freshness remain visible at every projection.** Distinguish content/publication time, last successful retrieval and latest attempt/error. Preserve the last good snapshot during refresh and failure; show cached/stale plus its real successful timestamp in Local. No network image or embedded reader asset may silently refresh a cached view. Feed disable, mute, remove-from-Screen and delete-local-record have different effects and labels. Reader/reminder links retain publisher, source URL and source item identity. Briefs retain item-level provenance rather than presenting one global timestamp as proof that every section is fresh.
7. **Extend one Your Day experience.** Home already has a daily summary. Its Screen/brief view should share canonical snapshot and action routing, with morning/on-demand/evening and comparison-to-baseline semantics. No baseline is different from “nothing changed.” Deterministic results work locally; optional model wording cannot invent facts, hide stale status or change action authority. Quiet hours govern proactive brief delivery; do not silently treat them as an alarm cancellation policy.
8. **Screen layout and launcher widget instances are different owners.** Screen cards use stable `presentationId`; launcher instances use Android `appWidgetId`. Each instance persists its own size, city/time zone, source/filter and privacy configuration. Reorder/resize/hide edits preview and explicitly Apply/Cancel, with a current-version check and accessible button/keyboard equivalents. Removing a card never deletes its task, event, feed or source. Opening an instance resolves its current canonical record; a deleted/missing record has an honest recovery state.
9. **Launcher widgets are constrained projections.** Use the same data/state/action vocabulary and theme assets within launcher limitations, not a miniature duplicated app or pretend permanently running Screen. Personal content needs a redacted projection independent of Activity-window flags. Time labels must communicate refresh constraints; resize may shorten the item list, not remove the only action/source/state label. Reconfigure affects one instance. Source/locale/theme/mode/profile changes must update or clear prior published content appropriately.
10. **Child presentation retains child authority.** Kids/Study inherit the two global modes but do not gain adult navigation, unapproved source browsing or parent controls. Serve an age-appropriate brief through an explicitly allowed child owner. Global Power is not proof that every public news item is appropriate for a child. Use the existing scoped parent gate/content boundaries; maintain 56dp child actions and the permitted compact placement. Do not obtain this by admitting all Screen owners to Kids.

## Shared presentation details to preserve

- `HaloAlertDialog` captures the actual reading density in every dialog slot. Use scrollable bodies for long recurrence/source configuration; keep action labels fully readable at large text.
- `ScreenContinuation.kt:22` already revalidates source identity before opening. Extend that owner for organizer/feed records; the displayed card is not authority to open a deleted, replaced or newly restricted record.
- `HaloFeedbackSurface` uses polite live-region semantics; avoid repeated refresh announcements or a clock that speaks every tick. Dynamic data updates must preserve focus and list position.
- Reuse semantic `HaloGlyph`/Zone 5 assets and visible names. Weather, task completion, source state and permission state must not rely on colour alone. Create missing assets only for real new concepts, with matching static/reduced-motion states.
- Keep updated/failed refreshes from producing fake success motion. Owner-confirmed completion controls receipts and guided-step completion. Guidance pauses during input, review, active task and parent disclosure.
- A cached news reader or brief must remain readable after a failed refresh and must label a deliberate external-open action. Independent task entry/editing remains enabled when weather/news or optional models are unavailable.
