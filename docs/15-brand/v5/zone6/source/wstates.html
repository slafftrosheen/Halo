halo · new look · widgets · sizes & states

Every size, every state, still honest

Widgets resize from 2×1 to 4×4. Small sizes keep one fact; big sizes add context. When data is old, missing or text is large, the widget says so and stays usable.

Small keeps one fact

2×1 shows the single most useful line: the time, the next event, the running timer.

Old data says so

“Updated 2 h ago · can’t reach the source” in amber, plus Try again. It never pretends to be live.

Large text, fewer lines

At large text the widget drops to its essentials instead of shrinking letters.

Light & accents

Widgets follow halo’s Appearance — light or dark, any accent — so the home screen matches the app.
