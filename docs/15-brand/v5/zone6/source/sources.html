halo · new look · sources → widgets

Everything you follow, gathered on your phone

halo fetches every source you choose — weather, news, official alerts, calendars, plus your own organiser, notes and activity — and lays it out in widgets. Time and date are always there; the rest appears only when a source has delivered.

One place to manage

The Widgets page lists every source with its real status — “Updated 9:38”, “Paused”, “Can’t reach” — and which widgets it feeds.

Your day in halo

The big widget joins it all: weather, the next event, open tasks, alerts and what halo did for you — one calm glance.

Freshness, honestly

Every widget shows when its data arrived. Stale data says so; nothing is shown as live when it isn’t.

Alerts stand out — gently

Official warnings use Apricot and rise to the top of Your day. Everything else stays calm.
