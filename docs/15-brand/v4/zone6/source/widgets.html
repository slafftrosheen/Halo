halo · new look · home-screen widgets

Screen moved to your home screen

Organiser, sources, news, clocks and the brief now live as Android widgets. Same calm glass, same open ring, same honest states — and every widget has a small ring to ask halo about it.

Glance, then go

Each widget answers one question. A tap opens the exact item in halo — the organiser entry, the article, the timer.

Ask about this

The small ring on every widget starts halo with that widget as context: “What do I need for 14:00?”

Honest when empty

No source yet? The widget says so and offers one fix — “Choose a weather source” — instead of showing made-up data.

Follows your look

Accent, light / dark and text size follow halo’s Appearance. Apricot appears only when something needs you.
