# HPS-999: The Halo Constitution

## The Ten Immutable Principles

1. **Halo is local-first.**
2. **Users own their data.**
3. **No mandatory accounts.**
4. **No vendor lock-in.**
5. **Every external dependency is replaceable.**
6. **Privacy is visible, not assumed.**
7. **Every capability is inspectable.**
8. **Hardware is abstracted.**
9. **Interfaces are stable.**
10. **Simplicity beats cleverness.**
